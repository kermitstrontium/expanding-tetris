ExpandingTETRIS

Every four-line clear pushes the walls out.

How to play

Clear lines as usual. Clear four at once and the field gets one column wider on each side. Wider fields score the same per line but take more blocks to fill, and the view zooms out so the whole field always fits. It grows taller too, keeping the classic 1:2 shape, so pieces get longer to travel. Drag slowly for fine moves; swipe fast to cross a wide field.
